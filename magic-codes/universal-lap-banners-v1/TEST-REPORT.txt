SCOPE AND BEHAVIOR
Adds original LAP artwork and existing HUD numerals on intermediate laps
3-98 of standard lap races with 4-99 total laps. Native Lap 2 and the actual
Final Lap remain untouched. No new audio call or audio asset is included.

The common lap HUD is called by ordinary, boss, Taj and Time Trial race
paths. The feature has no map or vehicle allowlist. Non-lap challenge/hub
paths do not qualify. Online and split-screen are deliberately excluded.
This source-level coverage does not mean every course has been driven.
Other mods that replace the same HUD hook may conflict.

The code does not set race length, replace lap counters, edit racer state,
read native lap timing arrays, or change trophy results. It uses a separate
presentation clock and private copies of the native HUD elements.
